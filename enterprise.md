## FAQ

<!-- widget:accordion -->

### How is Enterprise priced?

It's custom: a flat price for your team, agreed on the call. Prices are in US dollars and billed through Paddle, like Pro.

### Does everyone on the team need a plan?

No. On Enterprise the project has one balance, and every teammate with access spends it.

### Can we use our own AI provider?

Yes. Add an OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic or Vercel AI Gateway key on **Settings ▸ Agent ▸ Your own AI API Key**; your provider bills those calls and Docsbook charges nothing for them.

### Can we try Docsbook before talking to sales?

Yes. Every account starts with a [14-day Pro trial](./pricing/plans.md) with starter AI credit and no card.
