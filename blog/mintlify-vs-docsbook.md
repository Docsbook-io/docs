# Mintlify vs Docsbook: Which Documentation Platform Is Right for You?

Mintlify is a popular documentation platform for Y Combinator startups and fast-growing SaaS companies. It's AI-first and used by developer teams. But is it the right choice for every company?

In this post, we break down the key differences between Mintlify and Docsbook to help you make an informed decision.

## Pricing: The Elephant in the Room

Mintlify's starter plan starts at **$150/month**. For early-stage startups and indie developers, that's a significant commitment before you've validated your product.

Docsbook starts at **$59/month** — the same features, without the enterprise-tier price tag. You pay for what you need, when you need it.

## Setup Experience

Both platforms are GitHub-native. You connect your repo, and documentation deploys automatically on every push. The difference is in the details:

**Mintlify** requires a `mint.json` configuration file with a specific schema. It's powerful but adds friction.

**Docsbook** reads your Markdown files as-is. Your existing `README.md` and folder structure work out of the box. No config file required on day one.

## AI Search: Under the Hood

Mintlify's AI search supports natural language queries and surfaces answers from across your docs.

Docsbook's AI search generates citations, works with multi-language docs, and is accessible via API — so you can embed it into your product, not just your docs site.

## SEO: Where Docsbook Wins

Documentation pages are some of the highest-value SEO content a company can publish. "How to integrate X" and "API reference for Y" are phrases developers search constantly.

Mintlify generates fast pages, but SEO customization is limited to basic meta tags.

Docsbook is built SEO-first:
- Structured data (JSON-LD) for every page
- Customizable meta titles and descriptions per page
- `llms.txt` support for AI crawler discoverability
- Sitemap generated and updated automatically
- Open Graph images auto-generated

## When to Choose Mintlify

- Your team already uses Mintlify and migration cost is high
- You need advanced MDX component customization
- Budget is not a constraint and you want the most mature ecosystem

## When to Choose Docsbook

- You're an early-stage startup watching burn rate
- You want docs live in 5 minutes, not an afternoon
- SEO and AI discoverability are part of your growth strategy
- You need multi-language documentation
- You want white-label docs without enterprise pricing

## Conclusion

Mintlify is a strong product. If you can afford it and are deep in their ecosystem, it delivers. For teams that want quality documentation without enterprise pricing, Docsbook is the more cost-effective choice.
