# Read Time Analytics

Find out how long visitors actually spend on each page — not just whether they opened it.

## What It Shows

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Average read time | Mean time spent on a page per visit |
| Pages by engagement | Pages ranked from most to least read |

## Why It Matters

Page views tell you what people open. Read time tells you what they actually read.

- **Low read time** on a complex page → content may be unclear, too long, or poorly structured.
- **High read time** on a short page → visitors may be confused and rereading.
- **Consistent read time** across a tutorial → readers are progressing through it as intended.

Use this data to prioritize which pages to rewrite first.

## How to Access

1. Open your docs site.
2. Float Widget → **Analytics** tab.
3. Select the **Read Time** view.

> **Pro feature.** Read Time Analytics requires a Pro plan.
